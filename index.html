About Me

Grounding molecular biology & laboratory rigor into computational inquiry

Dedicated Biotechnology Graduate with Research & Computational Focus

I am a Biotechnology graduate holding dual Bachelor’s degrees from October University for Modern Sciences and Arts (MSA), Egypt, and the University of Greenwich, UK. My academic and research formation combines comprehensive wet-lab proficiencies across molecular biology, microbiology, and bioprocessing with analytical capabilities in biological sequence analysis and computational biology.

Throughout my degree, I have led two major graduation investigations as First Author (both currently under peer review for publication): engineering a novel Cu-BiOBr 3D Porous Sponge for heavy metal wastewater remediation (awarded Grade A+), and elucidating the physiological and biochemical modulations of gamma irradiation on eggplant fruit.

My current academic aspiration is centered on bioinformatics, computational biology, sequence alignment, and multiomics. I actively utilize biological sequence analysis tools (BLAST, BioEdit, SnapGene, MEGA, Clustal Omega), navigate biological databases (NCBI, Ensembl, UniProt), and leverage AI-assisted programming alongside coursework in Python, R, and C++ to bridge computational logic with real-world biological systems.

Cu-Doped Bismuth Oxybromide (Cu-BiOBr) 3D Porous Sponge for Enhanced Lead (Pb²⁺) Adsorption from Wastewater

Problem & Research Question

Industrial effluents frequently discharge toxic divalent lead (Pb²⁺) into aquatic ecosystems, posing severe biological risks. Traditional adsorbents face kinetics limitations and poor reusability. The research addressed: How can copper doping modulate the electronic structure of bismuth oxybromide (BiOBr) within a 3D porous sponge scaffold to dramatically accelerate Pb²⁺ adsorption capacity?

Approach & Methodology

Synthesized Cu-doped BiOBr nanostructures through solvothermal synthesis; integrated the resulting composite into a durable 3D porous sponge matrix. Executed adsorption equilibrium runs, varying contact time, dosage, and metal concentration, and performed optical characterization via UV-Vis-NIR spectrophotometry.

My Role

First Author & Lead Researcher: Executed solvothermal synthesis protocols, sample preparations, spectrophotometric measurements, adsorption isotherms/kinetics modeling, and authored the academic publication manuscript.

Key Skills & Techniques

Solvothermal Nanomaterial Synthesis 3D Porous Sponge Fabrication UV-Vis-NIR Spectrophotometry Heavy Metal (Pb²⁺) Adsorption Origin Data Fitting

Outcome & Achievement

Awarded an A+ (Excellent) grade by the faculty evaluation committee. The manuscript has been prepared and submitted for publication, currently Under Review.
